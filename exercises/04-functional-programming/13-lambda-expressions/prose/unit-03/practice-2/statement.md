A chess club's league page lists every player in one table. Players are grouped by division, with
the divisions in alphabetical order. Within a division the player with the most points comes
first. Players on equal points are listed by name, so the table doesn't reshuffle every time the
results feed refreshes and the players arrive in a different order.

`LeagueTable.sorted` already sorts with `standings()`. Write `standings()` in
`src/main/java/practice/LeagueTable.java`. About three lines in total.
