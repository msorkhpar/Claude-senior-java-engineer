The shop's loyalty service has always been right for ordinary members. Since histories of a
thousand entries or more started running on a parallel stream, the long-standing members complain:
one sees thousands of points she never earned, another a balance that has nothing to do with what
he redeemed.

`LoyaltyPoints` picks the stream for you. Finish the two `TODO`s in
`src/main/java/practice/LoyaltyPoints.java`:

- `earned(...)`: the welcome bonus, given once, plus the points of every purchase;
- `remaining(...)`: the opening balance less every redemption.

Each must give the same answer whether the stream it gets is sequential or parallel. About two
lines in total.
