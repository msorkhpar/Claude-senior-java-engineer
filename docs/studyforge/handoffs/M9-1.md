## The proposal (reconnaissance)

The survey measured the following (17 measurements, 11 open questions):

- **Curriculum record:** `README.md`, in the `## Curriculum` region. It holds 10 sections, written as bare numbered lines (`1. Java Fundamentals`). Under them sit 45 modules, each a list entry linking `NN-dir/README.md`. Under the modules sit 166 lesson files, linked as `README_x.y.z.md`: 157 at the second indent and 9 at the third.
  - The 9 at the third indent are real lesson files nested under a lesson: 6.6.1.1 to 6.6.1.4 in `25-fork-join` and 7.3.2.1 to 7.3.2.5 in `29-date-time-api`.
  - A further 174 unlinked 4-part lines (such as `2.3.1.1.`) are headings inside lessons, not units.
- **Module `README.md` files** (45, 9 to 15 lines each) are contents pages that repeat the record. They are not lessons.
- **Proposed shape:** `levels: ["section", "module"]`, 10 sections, 45 modules and 166 units, one lesson document each. `variants: ["prose"]`, `exercises: true` (ruled: authored by coverage), `placement: "tree"` (ruled), `narration: true` and `media.commit: "never"` (ruled).
- **Not material:** `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, `LICENSE`, `.claude/**`, `.sdkman`, `.gitignore`, `scripts/**`, `00-base/**` (the shared test-utility module), `pom.xml`, the module contents pages, and each module's `pom.xml` and `src/**`. The Java sources and tests are not prose. They are the material the exercises step reads, and every example inside the lesson prose is kept.
- **Duplication:** none, neither whole-file nor structural.
- **Execution:** 47 build files, 169 files that look like graders and 178 other source files. The drafted runtimes are `java`, `maven` and `shell`.

## Questions for the user

Each question blocks this step. Q1 also blocks any ingest.

**Q1. The personal-data gate refuses example data in 13 lessons.**
- **What happens:** 17 code blocks in 13 of the 166 lesson files carry sample e-mail addresses (`alice@example.com`, `bob@test.com`, `a@b.com`, and others) or one sample Unix home-directory path (in `30-text-blocks/README_7.4.4`). The archive's personal-data gate refuses them and rewrites nothing. It even refuses reserved domains such as `x@example.invalid`, and the manifest has no exception.
- **Affected files:** `06-class-obj/README_2.1.2`, `09-records/README_2.4.3`, `14-functional-interfaces/README_4.2.4`, `15-method-references/README_4.3.1` and `README_4.3.3`, `16-streams-api/README_4.4.4`, `27-modern-java-overview/README_7.1.3`, `30-text-blocks/README_7.4.4` and `README_7.4.5`, `31-solid-principles/README_8.1.1`, `34-composition-inheritance/README_8.4.3`, `36-creational-patterns/README_9.1.3`, `37-structural-patterns/README_9.2.2`.
- **Options:**
  - (a) A framework row lets reserved example domains (RFC 2606 and 6761) and a documented placeholder home path through. The course then edits only the samples that use real registrable domains (`test.com`, `co.com`, `x.com`, `b.com`, `d.org`).
  - (b) A framework row adds a manifest-declared allowlist of literal sample strings, each with a reason.
  - (c) Only the course changes: all 17 samples are edited to placeholders the current gate accepts. This is a content edit in 13 lesson files.
  - (d) Exclude the 13 files. That loses material and breaks "keep every example".
- **Recommendation:** (a). The examples stay examples. The fix lands in the framework, and the course edit is small.

**Q2. How the course is filed (depth and levels).**
- **What happens:** the manifest's `curriculum` block cannot declare this record's section, then module, then unit shape. Declaring the 45 modules is refused ("10 labels, corpus.json declares 45"), and declaring the 10 sections would file the module contents pages as units.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Depth 2, `section` and `module`, with `curriculum: {record}` only and the filing written in `ingest/read.py`. This is the rehearsal's shape, and it validates.
  - (b) As (a), but a framework row first lets `curriculum` declare nested groups. The filing then moves into `corpus.json`.
  - (c) Depth 1: 45 modules as containers, and the sections are lost.
  - (d) Depth 1: 10 sections as containers, and the modules are lost.
- **Recommendation:** (a) now and (b) filed as a framework finding. Moving from (a) to (b) later changes only `corpus.json` and `read.py`; the archive keeps the same shape. Also confirm the level names `section` and `module`: the course itself says "module" (in `CLAUDE.md` and `PROGRESS.md`).

**Q3. Section addresses.** The record writes none, and an address is recorded, never derived.
- **Options:**
  - (a) `01-java-fundamentals`, `02-classes-and-objects`, … `10-advanced-java-topics`, proposed once and then recorded.
  - (b) `01` … `10`.
  - (c) Names the user gives.
- **Recommendation:** (a).

**Q4. The nested lessons** (6.6.1.1 to 6.6.1.4 and 7.3.2.1 to 7.3.2.5).
- **Options:**
  - (a) Units in the record's order, at the same level as their parent lesson.
  - (b) A third container level for these two modules only.
- **Recommendation:** (a). Their labels keep the 4-part number, so the page still shows the nesting.

**Q5. The 45 module contents pages** (`NN-dir/README.md`).
- **Options:**
  - (a) `not_material`: navigation that repeats the record.
  - (b) `exclude`, with a reason.
  - (c) A unit 0 per module.
- **Recommendation:** (a). The site's own contents replace them.

**Q6. Classification and reasons.** Every `not_material` glob needs a reason, and "a reason is a person's". The recommended texts are in `M9-1-rehearsal-draft.json` under `reasons`: 14 distinct texts over 146 globs.
- **Recommendation:** approve them as drafted, or edit them. This includes moving `CLAUDE.md` from `include` to `not_material`. The survey drafted it as material only because the overview links it above the curriculum.

**Q7. The `source` slug.** The survey drafted `claude-senior-java-engineer-interview-preparation`.
- **Recommendation:** keep it.

**Q8. `variants`.**
- **Options:** `["prose"]` (the survey's draft) or `["java"]` (as in the shipped depth-2 example).
- **Recommendation:** `["prose"]`. There is one rendering, and a variant is filing only.

**Q9. `runtimes`.**
- **Options:** the drafted `java`, `maven` and `shell`, or `java` and `maven`.
- **Recommendation:** `java` and `maven`. `shell` is evidenced only by the author's `scripts/validate-module.sh`. This matters from the execution step on, not for the reading site.

**Q10. The TREE ruling against what `tree` actually writes.**
- **What happens:** the ruling says everything generated lives under `.studyforge/`. Under `tree` the pages, assets and site cache do. But `corpus.json`, `archive/`, `index.html`, `ingest/` and `tests/` are written at the course root. The reader document may not be placed under `.studyforge/` (`corpus.md` refuses it). Lesson folders stay untouched either way.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Accept those root entries, and put the reader document at `docs/studyforge/ONBOARDING.md`.
  - (b) As (a), with `onboarding_doc: false`.
  - (c) A framework row for a generated root that holds all of it.
- **Recommendation:** (a), with (c) filed as a finding for the user to weigh.

**Q11. Where the built site is written.**
- **Options:**
  - (a) `studyforge build . --out .`: `index.html` at the root and pages under `.studyforge/`. This is the committed-site shape the narration release scripts assume.
  - (b) `--out .studyforge/site`.
- **Recommendation:** (a).
